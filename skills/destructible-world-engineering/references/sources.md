# Official references and limits

Rapier events: https://www.rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/advanced_collision_detection_js/ . Contact force events require ActiveEvents.CONTACT_FORCE_EVENTS and EventQueue; use the correct dt and do not confuse force (N) with impulse (N*s) or energy (J).
Read the installed versions of rapier-physics-3d and planck-custom-physics-2d before editing collision integration.
Three.js geometry and collider assets are owned by game renderer/physics layers, not this model.
